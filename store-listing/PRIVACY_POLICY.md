# Open Wire — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

Open Wire is a news reader built around one idea: pull in public RSS and
news feeds from around the world, and let you read them without creating
an account or handing anything over. This page explains exactly what the
app does and doesn't do with your data, in plain language, with nothing
left out. (This is the same text shown in-app under Settings → Privacy
policy — keep both in sync if either changes.)

## The short version

- **No account, ever** — there's no sign-up, login, or user ID of any
  kind. Nobody can tie anything the app does back to you by name.
- **Nothing leaves your phone** — your saved articles, region choice, and
  settings are stored only on your device, not on any server we run.
- **Location, used once** — approximate location is read once on first
  launch, to pick a default country, then never requested again unless
  you reset it.
- **No ads, no selling data** — Open Wire has no advertising SDK and we
  don't sell, rent, or trade data to anyone, for any reason.

## What the app accesses, and why

**Internet access.** Open Wire fetches publicly published RSS and Atom
feeds (for example, BBC, Al Jazeera, Times of India, CBC, ABC, and Google
News) directly from each publisher's own servers, plus their article
pages when a feed doesn't include a preview photo. These requests go
straight from your device to that publisher, the same way your phone's
browser would reach their website. Each publisher's own privacy policy
governs that connection, since Open Wire is only the app making the
request, not a server sitting in between.

**Approximate location.** If you grant it, Open Wire reads your device's
coarse (network-based, not GPS-precise) location one time, right after
install, purely to set your default country feed. That location is
converted to a country on your device and is never transmitted anywhere,
stored in any file we control, or logged. You can deny the permission
with no loss of functionality beyond needing to pick a region yourself;
you can also change your region at any time from within the app.

**Notifications.** If you enable them, new-story alerts are generated
entirely on your device by periodically re-checking your own feeds in the
background. No notification content is generated or routed through any
server we operate.

## What we store, and where

- **On your device only:** your chosen region and category filters, your
  saved/bookmarked articles, your notification preference, and a
  short-lived local cache of recent headlines so the app opens instantly.
  Uninstalling the app removes all of it.
- **Crash and usage data:** Open Wire may use Google Firebase to receive
  anonymous crash reports and basic, aggregate usage signals (such as
  which screens are opened or which app version is running), so we can
  find and fix bugs and understand which features are actually used.
  This data is not linked to your name, email, or any account, because no
  account exists. Firebase's own privacy practices are described at
  https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
- **Nothing else.** We don't collect contacts, photos, messages, browsing
  history outside the app, or any other device data.

## Third-party content

Every headline, summary, and photo you see is published by the original
news outlet, not by us. Tapping a story opens that outlet's own article,
and from that point you're subject to their site and their policies, same
as visiting it directly in a browser. Open Wire doesn't host, edit, or
claim ownership of any of this content — it's a reader, not a publisher.

## Children's privacy

Open Wire is a general news app, not directed at children, and we don't
knowingly collect any information from anyone under 13. If you believe a
child has provided us information, contact us below and we'll address it.

## Your choices

- Deny or revoke location access any time in your device's app settings
  — the app still works, you'll just pick a region manually.
- Turn notifications off from within the app or your device settings.
- Clear saved articles and local cache by clearing the app's storage, or
  simply by uninstalling it.

## Changes to this policy

If what the app collects or how it's used changes meaningfully, this
page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change.
Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Play Store release
notes.
