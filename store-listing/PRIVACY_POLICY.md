# Open Wire — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

Open Wire is a news reader published by Sriram Anappindi.

## What the app does

Open Wire fetches public RSS/Atom news feeds directly from their original
publishers (for example, BBC News, Al Jazeera, TechCrunch, ScienceDaily)
and displays their headlines, short excerpts, and links back to the
original articles on the publisher's own site.

## What data we collect

Open Wire does not require an account and does not collect, store, or
transmit any personal data to us. Specifically:

- We do not collect your name, email address, or any identifying
  information.
- We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.
- We do not use advertising identifiers or show ads.
- Headlines you view are fetched and cached only on your own device, for
  offline access; this cache never leaves your device and we never see
  it.

## Network access

The app connects directly, from your device, to the public news feeds
listed in the app, in order to download headlines. It also opens your
device's browser when you tap a story, to take you to the original
article. Those publishers' own privacy policies govern what happens once
you're on their site.

Your device's standard network connection (Wi-Fi/mobile data) is used
only to fetch these feeds and open article links; no analytics or
tracking network calls are made by the app itself.

## Children's privacy

Open Wire is not directed at children and collects no personal data from
anyone, including children.
