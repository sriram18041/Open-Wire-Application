## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Open Wire keeps one continuously-updating feed of real headlines, pulled
straight from public news sources — no login, no paywall, no algorithm
deciding what you don't get to see.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• World and politics from global wire services
• Business and markets
• Technology
• Science
• Sports
• Entertainment and culture

HOW IT WORKS
Open Wire fetches headlines directly from the original publishers' own
feeds. Tap any story and it opens the full article at its real source —
Open Wire never rewrites or republishes full articles itself, it's a
reader, not a copy.

BUILT FOR ACTUALLY READING THE NEWS
• Filter by category or region
• Search across every headline at once
• Pull to refresh, or let it refresh itself in the background
• Works offline from your last refresh — no blank screen on the subway

PRIVACY
Open Wire doesn't require an account, doesn't track you across apps, and
doesn't sell data — there's no data to sell. See the privacy policy link
on this listing for specifics.
